About Us

Who We Are

EditPro was created to help content creators improve their video editing skills through simple tutorials, trending editing ideas, and AI-powered support. Our goal is to make high-quality editing accessible to everyone, whether you're just starting or already creating videos for thousands of viewers. It was created in 2026 by Senuja De Silva, a Year 9 Computer Technology student at Tempe High School. Developed as part of the Introduction to Software Engineering course, the website showcases skills in Python Flask, Bootstrap, website planning, and documentation while helping users create better video edits through tutorials and editing resources.

We believe that anyone can create amazing content with the right guidance. That's why EditPro provides step-by-step lessons, editing tips, popular effects, and recommendations for trending sounds all in one place.

Our mission is to inspire creativity, save creators time, and make learning video editing fun and easy. I have always been into editing and making fun videos ever since I was a kid, so this website would've been perfect for me to use back then.
